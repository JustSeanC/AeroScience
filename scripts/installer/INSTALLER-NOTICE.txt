AeroScience - Live Flight Recording Alpha

Setup installs AeroScience for your Windows account, creates a Start menu shortcut, offers a desktop shortcut and registers an uninstaller in Windows Installed Apps.

The self-contained companion, authorized native MSFS 2024 SimConnect runtime, SDK-compiled Community toolbar and Microsoft Visual C++ prerequisite are included. A missing Visual C++ prerequisite may require Windows administrator approval. Its shared runtime is retained when AeroScience is uninstalled.

After installation, guided setup lets you independently choose mission database and scientific library locations, review drive capacity and approve or skip toolbar installation. Close MSFS before changing its Community folder. Existing locations are preselected on upgrades. Requested data moves use verified copies and retain originals.

Scientific mapping and remote-sensing missions may generate substantial amounts of data. Storage requirements will vary by instrument, recording settings, and mission duration. Consider using a dedicated high-capacity drive.

Scientific instruments and their acquisition formats are future work. No placeholder scientific observations are generated by setup.

Uninstall removes application files and shortcuts. Mission history, scientific datasets and settings are retained. It separately asks whether to remove the AeroScience toolbar; unrelated Community add-ons are preserved.
