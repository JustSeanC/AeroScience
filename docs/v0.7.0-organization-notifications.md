# Organization Discord notifications

Notifications are optional and OFF by default. Each independent organization can save one Discord incoming webhook destination. AeroScience does not run a bot or a central notification service.

## Administrator

Open **Organization Settings → Notifications** in the organization portal. Complete **Account Security** first: an authenticator, confirmed saved recovery codes and recent MFA are required for changes, tests, disclosure and cancellation.

Enter a **Destination name** and the Discord incoming webhook in the password field. Choose the categories, enable notifications and select **Save Notification Settings**. The saved view shows only the name and masked identifier. Saving sends no test. Open **Preview Test Notification**, then explicitly choose **Send Test Notification** if wanted. **Refresh Delivery Status** reads actual durable delivery state.

**Review External Announcements** shows current supported research approvals and documented exceptions. Read the exact preview, confirm that it may appear in an external channel, then choose **Approve External Announcement**. Organization membership visibility alone does not authorize public disclosure. Raw measurements, pilot notes and reviewer comments are excluded. Client-reported attempts/evaluations and evidence receipts are not approved results and cannot be announced as completion. Unsupported complete scientific products remain unsupported.

To rotate a credential, enter the replacement and save. To stop notifications, clear **Enable Discord notifications** and save. **Delete Destination** removes the saved protected credential and cancels pending intents after confirmation. Every settings revision invalidates prior destination-bound pilot/disclosure consent. It cannot recall a post already sent to Discord or reliably cancel a request already in progress. Revoke an exposed webhook in Discord too. Existing encrypted disaster-recovery backups follow their separate retention policy; deletion here is not a promise to erase historical operator-controlled backups or Discord posts.

## Pilot

Start recording and share a flight normally in AeroScience. Member-visible Live Flights does not permit Discord announcements.

Choose **My Discord Announcements** in My Research → Organizations to open that organization's portal. Sign in if necessary. Under **My Discord Flight Announcements**, inspect the destination, policy revision and exact current flight. **Allow Discord Announcements for This Flight** separately authorizes the public flight label, start/end-sharing event and UTC time. A Discord channel may include nonmembers. Coordinates, tracks, private local mission names, notes and scientific data are never included.

**Revoke Discord Announcements** stops unsent announcements. Already acknowledged posts remain in Discord. Destination changes require new consent. The flight sharing grant alone cannot approve or modify Discord consent. Each organization and flight is independent. No ordinary member, pilot, reviewer or manager can configure webhooks.

## Delivery meaning

- **Pending:** retained intent, awaiting current permission checks and permitted send time.
- **Sending:** dispatch started; delivery is not yet confirmed.
- **Delivered:** Discord returned a valid message identity with `wait=true`. This is not scientific approval.
- **Failed:** definitive rejection or exhausted rate-limit retry budget. Rejected destinations are disabled.
- **Disabled:** cancelled, changed destination, revoked consent/authority, or no longer eligible.
- **Ambiguous:** a timeout, interrupted acknowledgment, unexpected response or restart during dispatch means Discord might have received the message. No automatic retry occurs. Inspect the actual channel before any independent new test/action. **Cancel Delivery** can close an unresolved intent, not delete a Discord post.

Delivery history is paged. An eligible event is scheduled at most once per destination revision. Five rate-limit attempts maximum; response-provided delay and bounded exponential backoff are respected. A single destination serializes sends at least two seconds apart; at most 500 unfinished intents are processed. This is a processing bound, not a lifetime history cap. Existing recording, live monitoring, submissions and scientific results are independent of Discord availability.

## Acceptance boundaries

Automated engineering uses isolated PostgreSQL, real organization/Engine processes and a controlled HTTPS receiver. It does not prove an actual Discord channel, installed MSFS, Railway deployment, external disaster recovery or novice hosting acceptance. Those operational checks remain in P28/P29. No owner test is required during P27 engineering.
