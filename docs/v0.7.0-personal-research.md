# Personal Research — Flight-Regime Validation

Your experiments stay on your computer. You do not need an account or internet access.

1. Open **My Research**. Under **Choose an Experiment**, find **Flight-Regime Validation**. Click **Fly Now** to prepare an example, or **Customize** to change your own copy in Mission Planner. Review the speed, altitude and hold time for your simulated aircraft; the examples do not suit every aircraft.
2. For a customized plan, click **Save mission plan**, then **Select for Flight**. In Mission Control, **Your Research Flight** explains what to do. Use the normal **Start mission** button, then **Activate Card**, **Begin Attempt** and **End Attempt**. Nothing starts automatically. **Deactivate Card** lets you switch targets without stopping flight or science recording.
3. Use **End mission** when finished. Open **My Research → Review Recorded Flight** or **Mission History → View mission**. In **Test-card Activity & Evidence Timeline**, click **Evaluate Attempt** or **Show Recorded Evidence**. Each attempt keeps its own card and timestamps. The original postflight UTC-interval evaluation is also available.

You can prepare a plan while disconnected. Connect MSFS and load an aircraft before recording. **Review Science and Storage Readiness** uses the existing advisory; normal startup checks still apply. This card needs flight telemetry, not an extra instrument or external dataset. Routes and other scientific instruments remain optional and keep their own requirements.

**Customize** keeps your original experiment and past results unchanged. Its **Your Experiment Targets** section edits a separate copy inside the full Mission Planner. You can still add routes, patterns, instruments or other cards. A failed save keeps your changes for retry. If you decline to replace an unsaved Planner draft, that draft stays in place. **Back to My Research** returns to your experiments. Saved cards also offer Fly Now and Customize; manual **Save Test Card** and **Open in Mission Planner** remain available.

**Pass** means the recorded speed and altitude stayed inside both tolerances for one continuous hold. **Fail** means enough evidence was recorded, but no hold met both targets. **Insufficient Evidence** means the recording cannot establish the result. Pauses, gaps and missing values do not count as a successful hold.

Speed is indicated airspeed in knots. Altitude is feet above mean sea level (MSL). Evidence times are UTC. Science-session controls remain separate. These simulator results do not certify an aircraft or physical instrument.

**Edit Card → Save New Revision** keeps old flights and results unchanged. Each flight uses the card revision selected before recording. Results preserve the flight, time range and original sample references. A result from demo telemetry is labeled as demo evidence.

## A short Windows check, when you next fly

- Open **My Research** and choose **Fly Now** under Flight-Regime Validation. You should see **Your Research Flight** with targets; recording should not start.
- Return with **Back to My Research**, then choose **Customize**. Change a target and click **Save mission plan**. Your other saved experiments should keep their old targets.
- If something looks wrong, stop that check and send a screenshot with what you expected.

No terminal commands or developer setup are part of this check. Automated tests cover the local workflow; an actual Windows/MSFS flight has not been claimed for this new feature.

## Understanding comparisons

Overview counts cover every retained flight for that experiment. **In Progress**, **Awaiting Evaluation** and **Interrupted** are different from a saved Pass, Fail or Insufficient Evidence result. Clicking Evaluate again preserves the evaluations without counting another marked attempt. Postflight intervals may overlap marked attempts; they are not independent trials.

Each comparison uses that attempt's original revision, targets and hold duration. A blue required-hold bar and a separately labeled recorded-hold bar have matching numbers in the accessible table. Missing, insufficient or changed evidence has no achieved-hold bar. A saved Pass from demo telemetry remains demo evidence. No statistical trend, aircraft certification or physical scientific-source acceptance is inferred.

**Refresh Experiment** clears the comparison selection and reloads current records. **Saved Local Results — All Evaluations** keeps earlier evaluations available. The work remains offline and sends no personal research to an organization.
