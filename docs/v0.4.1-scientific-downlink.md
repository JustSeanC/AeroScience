# Scientific downlink

Flight recording and scientific review have separate completion states. When a real
scheduled remote-source attempt cannot find a suitable retained source, its exact
packet and configuration are retained in the mission archive's recovery queue.

After the flight, bounded background preparation may retrieve an eligible historical
source under existing science consent, network and storage limits. Reconstruction
requires the exact committed aircraft packet and matching active configuration.
The output is labeled **Post-flight derived reconstruction**. Flight time, historical
source observation time and processing time remain separate. Original in-flight
products are never replaced. Repeating recovery does not duplicate an acquisition.

Review Pause, Resume or Cancel in Science Workspace. Offline operation and unavailable
storage preserve originals and pending work. Resume does not override an intentional
science pause during the flight. Missing telemetry, absent atmosphere measurements,
unknown legacy configurations and nodata cannot become successful observations.

Maps, reports and exports are provisional while recovery is pending. The background
worker rebuilds bounded native composites and coverage after recovery. Unsupported
derived operations retain an explicit limitation; they never replace a measurement.
Processing identities include actual product hashes. Older artifacts remain available
with their original identities, so an earlier exported file is not silently rewritten.
Export again after review completes to include the updated scientific review state.
