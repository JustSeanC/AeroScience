# Your research flight

## Plan → Prepare → Fly → Review

Create a route in Mission Planner, choose your instruments, and save the plan.
Select **Prepare Science Data** when you want bounded historical source retrieval.
Review the separate privacy disclosure and transfer/storage allowances before approving.
Selecting a mission or opening its map never starts recording or scientific downloads.

Choose **Select for Flight**. Mission Control previews the route. Check the aircraft,
connection and instrument readiness, then explicitly choose **Start mission**.
Unplanned Flight remains available when you want to fly without a saved route.

## Check preparation

Open Download Activity from the sidebar, Mission Control or Planner. Each instrument
shows ready regions separately from ongoing or unavailable work. A source being Ready
does not mean every pixel is valid. Cloud and nodata masks still apply.

Pause/Resume/Cancel inside a mission card affect that mission's preparation. Global
background controls are labeled separately. Unknown transfer size means unknown
completion time; there is no invented countdown. You may leave the page and return.

## Fly and finish

The recorder continues independently of open displays. Essential telemetry and the
route stay on Mission Control. Expand Flight details for attitude/profile/position,
or Flight tools for guidance, environmental context and in-flight notes.

End the mission when finished. Flight completion and scientific review completion
are separate: eligible saved acquisition attempts may require post-flight processing.
Pending results and exports remain provisional. Missing atmospheric observations
and intentionally paused science are not reconstructed.

## View your results

Find the mission in Mission History and choose **Open Science Review** or **Replay**.
Science Map loads the whole selected geographic scope as an overview, with independent
instrument layers. Zoom for more detail or inspect any saved record in its accessible
archive browser. Analysis presents actual instrument findings, ranges and charts.

Historical satellite/elevation data describe an external Earth observation, not a
new measurement by the simulated aircraft. Keep its original observation date separate
from your flight date. Transparent gaps indicate missing or masked displayed support.

## Compare and export

Choose two recorded datasets in Compare. Check their units, historical dates and
quality. Shared scales make equivalent colors mean equivalent values only for matching
quantities. Numeric differences require the server's scientific compatibility checks
and common valid native cells. An explanation replaces unsupported arithmetic.

Results & Exports offers the research ZIP, flight/science tables and reports. Native
GeoTIFFs preserve scientific units, grids and nodata. Exports do not change originals.
Export again after provisional processing completes to include its later derived data.

## Offline access and support

Mission History, saved values, source metadata and exports remain available without
MSFS or Internet while the selected data drives are connected. Library contains reusable
historical sources; Science Workspace contains results associated with recorded flights.

If a drive is missing, reconnect it or review Storage settings. Do not replace your
archive with an empty directory. If the companion disconnects, reconnect the display;
saved records are not deleted because a browser loses its connection.

Help & Support contains offline guides, GitHub reporting, optional Discord and
**Collect Feedback / Logs** on the companion PC. Review geographic/private information
before sharing a report. No terminal, GIS preparation or manually written manifest
is required for the normal supported automatic-source workflow.
