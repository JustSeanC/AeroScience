# Organization template publishing and governance

**Template Proposals** is available to active managers and administrators. Pilots see
**My Template Proposals**, containing only their own proposals. A Reviewer role alone
does not grant proposal-management or publishing authority.

## Review a proposal

Open **Template Proposals** and **Review Proposal**. Inspect the full validated
definition, source declarations, model limitations and optional comparison with
supported built-in definitions. Choose **Request Changes**, **Reject Proposal** or
**Accept for Drafting**. Requests and rejections require an explanation. Decisions
append immutable history. Accepting creates a separate organizational copy; the
pilot's personal template and earlier proposal revisions remain unchanged.

## Prepare and publish a draft

Open **Research Drafts**. **Edit Research Draft** changes the same supported typed
criteria used by the existing engine, including quantitative conditions, model
parameters, sequence and repetitions. Optionally associate an existing program and
campaign using their names. Choose **Review Draft Changes**, then
**Save Research Draft**. Earlier published revisions remain available.

**Review Publication** shows the exact saved revision, definition hash and applicable
policy. Publication is separate from accepting a proposal or saving a draft.
**Published Templates** contains immutable organizational definitions only. Managers
can choose **Use in Campaign**, select a program and create a campaign or choose **Use in Existing Campaign** using the
existing campaign editor. Publish the campaign and its **Campaign Test Cards** using
the normal workflows. Their claims, preparation, recording and evidence paths use the
same supported definition; a template does not bypass campaign publication policy.

## Choose a policy

Only an active administrator can change **Publishing Policy**.

| Policy | Publication authority |
| --- | --- |
| Legacy (migration default, revision 0) | Existing administrator-only campaign publication and manager/admin opportunity publication stay unchanged. New reusable-template publication requires an administrator. |
| Direct Manager Publication | Managers/admins may deliberately publish supported drafts. |
| Administrator Approval Required | A different active administrator must approve the exact saved author/revision/hash under the current policy revision. Managers/admins may then publish that approved draft. |

Under the approval policy, choose **Request Publication Approval**. An independent
administrator opens **Publication Review**, chooses **Inspect Publication Draft** and
then **Approve Publication** or **Reject Publication**. Approval is a deliberate action
and rejection requires an explanation. The author cannot approve their own revision,
including when they hold the Administrator role. A changed draft, changed policy or
revoked approver invalidates approval. A separate **Publish Definition** action is
still required. Stale concurrent decisions fail instead of overwriting history.

**Template History**, **Proposal History** and **Publication History** retain original
versions and publication authorization, including separate author, approver and
publisher identities. Queries are paged; there is no lifetime limit on retained
proposal or publication history.

## Boundaries

Existing claims, active flights, frozen plans, attempts, submissions, reviews and
accepted science do not change when a template is revised. Claim-revision adoption
remains informational; no mid-flight or automatic adoption is added. A published
template does not approve a scientific result. Existing P17/P18 submission-product
limitations remain in place. Public hosting, backups and deployment acceptance are
separate work; P24 does not deploy a server.
