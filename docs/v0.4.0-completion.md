## Expanded-provider investigation and remaining limitations

[The addendum report](v0.4.0-scientific-source-expansion.md) satisfies the required architecture/access investigation and distinguishes conditional preferred/optional work. A genuine NOAA EPT/LAZ node was independently decoded, but application guided point-cloud import/visualization is not implemented: validated LAZ packaging, hierarchy/classification handling, source dates and appropriate horizontal/vertical references remain work. Direct Hyperion individual-GeoTIFF product import needs a genuine authorized product and product-specific radiometry/wavelength/QA handling; it is not accepted by the existing ENVI wizard. WorldCover categorical analysis, GIBS layers and GOES processing are research only. No instrument claims coverage from the presence of a catalog/profile.

Complex occlusion, calibrated environmental penalties, full scanner return physics, hardware failures/calibration, 3D cloud reconstruction, SAR processing, cross-mission inference and future hosted organization services are intentionally outside this release. Terrain-aware planning is bounded relative-AGL center-ray intersection; it is not automatic simulator/external datum alignment. Large/polar/date-line display cases have explicit bounds rather than a fabricated coarse science result.
