# AeroScience pilot guide

## Install

Use the latest published **AeroScience Setup** EXE from GitHub Releases. Follow the
storage and Community-toolbar wizard, then launch **Start → AeroScience**. It opens
the dashboard and local services. Ordinary pilots need no terminal, SDK, account or
manual server. v0.4.1 remains development until its scientific and installed checks pass.

## Plan, prepare, select

Create a plan in Mission Planner. Choose an objective, route/survey, payload profile
and intervals. Aviation planning uses NM, feet MSL and degrees true; scientific
geometry stays in meters internally. Straight Transect repeats the exact A/B line;
turn around and rejoin using procedures appropriate for your aircraft.

Choose **Prepare Science Data** explicitly. Read the separate scientific-data privacy
choice and allow bounded downloads if desired. One summary per instrument reports
its required regions. Ready sources do not guarantee cloud-free or complete pixels.
Unknown transfer sizes produce an unknown ETA, not a countdown. Advanced Download
Activity exposes regions, attempts and allowance details. Pause/Resume is mission-scoped.

Choose **Select for Flight**. Mission Control shows the selected route and preparation
before recording. Selection alone neither records nor downloads. Choose Unplanned
Flight if you want to record without a saved plan.

## Fly and collect

Connect MSFS and wait for fresh aircraft telemetry. Press **Start Mission** explicitly.
The recording freezes the chosen plan and payload. Use Science Collection to operate
instruments and pause science independently of flight telemetry. Missing readings are
reported, not replaced. The simulator toolbar uses the same recorder and plan identity;
Settings → MSFS Toolbar exposes separate CommBus diagnostics.

## Complete and review

End the flight recording. Eligible missed remote-source attempts may enter **Downlinking
Science Data**. This is historical geographic reconstruction using saved packets, not
an aircraft transmission or newly observed satellite scene. Pause, Resume and Cancel
are available. Replay is usable immediately; pending final science remains provisional.

Open Science Workspace for Science Map, Compare, Research Analysis and Results/Exports.
Use the mission/sensor context, native-cell inspection, legends and original source dates.
Atmosphere charts describe simulator packets. Library contains reusable sources; it is
separate from mission-acquired results. Search History to reopen a flight offline.

## Export and understand limitations

The research ZIP preserves telemetry, science, events, original-source identities/hashes,
derived products and scientific review state. Source files remain in your library.
GeoTIFFs retain numeric values, native resolution and nodata. External imagery is
historical Earth data, not a live aircraft camera; elevation rasters are not laser returns.
A repeated simulator flight over one source scene is not evidence of Earth change.
