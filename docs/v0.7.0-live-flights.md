# Live Flights and organization events

Live Flights is a read-only view of current information that a pilot deliberately shares with one organization. **Every active member** of that organization can watch: pilots, reviewers, managers and administrators. Pending, suspended, removed and signed-out accounts cannot watch.

## Share a flight

1. In **My Research → Organizations**, authorize the separate live-flight desktop permission in the organization browser. Earlier read, claim and evidence grants cannot read or send live locations.
2. Start recording normally. Choose **Share Live Flight** on the selected organization connection.
3. Read the preview, choose **Public flight label**, confirm the audience and select **Start Sharing**.
4. Use **Stop Sharing** at any time. Recording and science collection continue independently.

Sharing stays off until that explicit action. Each organization needs its own connection and flight consent. Starting another flight, signing in, claiming work or reopening AeroScience never starts sharing automatically. Sharing does not resume after a desktop/server restart. A disconnected relay holds no upload queue. Choose **Stop Sharing**, reconnect and review the preview before explicitly starting again. If the server could not confirm stop, its old session may need up to two minutes to expire.

The preview covers current aircraft position, heading, **feet MSL**, **knots IAS**, available aircraft type, original UTC/freshness and bounded organizational card/science-session health. It excludes private notes, scientific readings, flight tracks, source files and personal research archives. Current telemetry can be newer than a committed recording sample. Live state is **not submitted evidence, reviewer approval or aircraft certification**.

## Watch Live Flights

Sign in to the organization portal and choose **Live Flights**. Choose an aircraft in the list to inspect its current position and status, or use the common map. **Filter aircraft, pilot label or campaign** narrows the list. The keyboard-accessible list remains usable without the map.

The bundled map uses an offline reference grid, with no external tile requests. Points represent current aircraft locations, never scientific footprints, tracks or area coverage. Simulator and synthetic-fixture sources are labeled separately. Unavailable values stay unavailable.

Updates normally arrive every five seconds. A 15-second-old update is stale. After 30 seconds the location disappears from the server/view and the map resets away from an expired selected position. Failed viewer requests clear saved positions. Idle sessions expire after two minutes; consent lasts at most eight hours and never outlives its authorization. A server cannot retract information someone already saw; revocation prevents subsequent authorized reads, and polling viewers clear on their next check (normally within five seconds). If Stop cannot reach the server, sending stops locally and the last server position still expires within 30 seconds.

The live view shows current/open or recently observed local attempts and point outcomes. It does not calculate cumulative repetitions or certify an entire procedure. Use the existing local results and separately approved evidence workflow for complete history and scientific review.

## Administrator restrictions

An administrator opens **Live Flights → Live Monitoring Policy**. Disable **Enable organization Live Flights**, or select sensitive published campaigns and choose **Save Live Monitoring Policy**. Every change revokes previous sharing consent.

A pilot holding an active claim in a restricted campaign cannot share *any* flight with this organization while that restriction applies. This deliberately broad rule prevents bypassing a sensitive restriction by omitting campaign tags. It does not control the simulator or expose another organization's membership.

## Organization Events

**Organization Events** shows permitted historical transitions. A locally reported attempt, an evidence receipt, a requirement finding, an approved complete-card contribution and an administrative exception are different events. Events retain identifiers and authority, never live coordinates, private notes or raw measurements.

Private receipt/review/exception events remain limited to their contributor and authorized reviewers/managers. Ordinary members see privacy-safe permitted activity and genuinely approved-card publication events; other people's internal actor identifiers are redacted. An earlier approval event is historical: **Approved Results** remains authoritative for current acceptance after a later decision.

## Discord announcements

Member-visible live sharing grants no external disclosure permission. The optional **My Discord Announcements** link opens the authenticated organization portal for a separate per-flight choice. Administrators configure **Organization Settings → Notifications**, OFF by default. Read [notification help](v0.7.0-organization-notifications.md) for consent, exact approval previews and real delivery states. [P27-C validation](validation/v0.7.0-p27c.md) records its separate engineering acceptance; actual Discord and installed-MSFS checks remain later operational gates.

## P28 owner check — later, not needed now

1. Start a recorded flight and open **My Research → Organizations → Share Live Flight**.
2. Read the preview, check the audience box and choose **Start Sharing**.
3. On another active member's browser, choose **Live Flights** and select your public label.
4. Choose **Stop Sharing** in AeroScience. The marker should disappear while your recording continues.
5. End the flight normally. Nothing should be uploaded as scientific evidence automatically.

Engineering checks are automated. Real installed-MSFS, Railway networking, external recovery, actual hosting costs and the five-stage novice hosting trial remain P28/P29 gates. No owner account action is requested during this development checkpoint.
