# v0.4.0 instrument reference profiles

These versioned configurations describe modeled geometry and selected operating parameters. They do not reproduce hardware calibration, create missing source bands, increase satellite resolution or measure real laser returns. All compatible aircraft remain usable in Universal and Realistic modes.

| Profile, revision 1 | Documented parameters used | Explicit limits |
|---|---|---|
| NASA AVIRIS-NG nominal | 36° cross-track FOV (documented ±2°); 600 resolved elements; selected 100 lines/s within 10–100; nominal 380–2510 nm / 5 nm spectral sampling | Published IFOV text is malformed/ambiguous, so the IFOV field is **unspecified**. The thin strip uses documented nominal 1 mrad spatial sampling, not a validated optical IFOV. Available wavelengths come from the actual source. A saved acquisition is a sparse strip, not proof of continuous scanning. |
| MicaSense RedEdge-P multispectral | 49.6° × 38.3°, 1456 × 1088, 3.45 µm pixel pitch, 5.5 mm lens; blue/green/red/red-edge/NIR center wavelengths 475/560/668/717/842 nm and published widths | Panchromatic 2464 × 2056 / 44.5° × 37.7° / 10 mm specifications are documented separately. The supplied Sentinel workflow has no manufactured red-edge or pan band. Its native source response is not RedEdge calibration. |
| FLIR A655sc, 15°, 25°, 45° lenses | 640 × 480; respective FOV 15° × 11°, 25° × 19°, 45° × 34°; 7.5–14 µm | Landsat surface temperature remains an independently dated external product, not a FLIR airborne image. No hardware temperature accuracy or radiometric calibration is conferred. |
| RIEGL VQ-1560 II-S maximum effective rate | 58° effective swath; rectangular ±8° forward/backward edge envelope; maximum effective measurement rate 2 × 1.33 MHz | Published PRR is distinct from effective measurement rate. Documented total scan rate is 40–600 lines/s. Minimum range is 100 m; example reflectance-dependent operating heights are not universal limits. Density is an ideal planning calculation, not a count of observed points. Actual dual-channel scan geometry, reflectance, occlusion and return selection are not reconstructed. |

The catalog also includes explicitly generic/custom configurations with bounded FOV, mount, optional detector counts, line rate and effective measurement rate. Unknown parameters stay unknown. Edited reference specifications must identify themselves as custom. New missions/plans/presets retain their effective versioned profile, lens and mount; acquisitions retain their effective configuration and method. Old snapshots are read unchanged and do not acquire a newly invented profile retrospectively. Toolbar enable/interval changes preserve the effective profile.
