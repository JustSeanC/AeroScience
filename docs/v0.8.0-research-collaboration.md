# Research collaboration and scientific evidence

This bundled guide works offline. Opening help does not connect to a server, download scientific data, share a location or submit evidence. Personal research remains local and usable without an organization.

## Find research and prepare

Open Organizations → My Organizations. Connect only to a server you trust. Check its exact address and organization identity, then approve the matching browser code and the particular permission you need. Read-only access does not allow claims, submissions or live locations. Membership and permissions can expire or be revoked.

Use Available Research to inspect the question, actual instrument/flight conditions, evidence requirements, pilot/contribution slots and published revision. Claim/accept the original card, then use My Assignments → Prepare Mission. The issued revision stays frozen; a newer publication does not silently change it. Advanced planning, custom research, revision history and campaign controls remain available. Releasing a claim is separate from deleting personal work.

Focus chooses the card you inspect. Activation, recording an attempt, local evaluation and completion retain their separate rules. A local pass is not an approved organization contribution. Campaign progress and exceptions use the organization's authoritative review and phase policies.

## Recording and science

Start Recording records the flight. Science collection is a separate authorized action. Armed means waiting for a configured trigger; paused means scientific collection is paused. Recording can continue while science is paused. Check the confirmed collection phase, session, actual retained observations, instrument/source readiness and quality warnings. Neither a click nor aircraft movement establishes successful acquisition.

An instrument can be operating while readings are unavailable, rejected or unassessed. Missing sources, calibration problems, gaps and original quality masks remain limitations. A retry does not create missing observations or repair scientific validity by itself.

## Four distinct scientific materials

- **Original AeroScience observations:** retained in-flight evidence with original flight/session identity, timestamps, hashes and quality.
- **Modeled scanner corridor:** predicted footprint geometry from retained aircraft/configuration inputs. It is neither measured terrain nor proof of coverage.
- **Source-backed DEM/DSM products:** derived from genuine retained terrain datasets. A DEM describes the declared terrain elevation surface; a DSM can include vegetation/structures. The source declaration, native units, date and height reference determine interpretation.
- **Historical LiDAR products:** genuine historical source-project points when original files and processing verify. The simulated aircraft did not measure these points. Classification, original coordinates/attributes, source identity and processing remain independently labeled.

Derived products are not new original acquisitions. They cannot increase original counts, grades, card credit or approved campaign progress. A flight track, source boundary or predicted overlap cannot fill a missing acquisition, NoData cell or unavailable source area. Indexed COPC and unvalidated CRS/vertical transforms remain unsupported.

## Dates, heights and provenance

Historical source acquisition, flight, retrieval/import and processing times describe different events. Unknown acquisition dates remain unknown; simulator time cannot replace them. A vertical datum defines the reference for height. Equal units do not make different or unknown datums compatible, and unverified heights must not be presented as aircraft clearance. Review source/project, native CRS, datum, original hashes, algorithm and scientific limitations in Results → Evidence.

Byte verification establishes identity and integrity, not suitability, correctness of an interpretation or scientific approval. A recipient can verify the received package and reference bytes. A supplied hash cannot verify an original source file that was not shared.

## Review and approve an exact submission

Review completed research in Results Overview / Explore / Evidence or My Research. Open the existing submission builder, select the completed card result and check its exact organization/server, campaign, assignment and issued revisions. Choose a browser-approved Submit Evidence connection and inspect the explicit contents, original notes/locations, actual package size or unknown-size notice, privacy disclosure and limitations.

Eligible P06 supporting attachments are explicitly selected **metadata/hash references**, versioned separately from original evidence. They identify modeled-corridor, source-backed terrain or historical LiDAR products and their verified dependencies. They do **not** contain DEM/DSM cells, point records, source binaries or numerical profile arrays. Only supported issued research contexts and receivers can accept them. Source exclusions, deletion, changed hashes or unavailable originals prevent approval/delivery. Unsupported peers do not silently downgrade a selected package; remove references explicitly and review a new selection if necessary. Complete terrain/raster/model-product scientific approval remains unsupported.

Saving a draft does not authorize transmission. Final approval binds exact content and recipient. Approved/queued, sending, retry/held, server receipt, awaiting scientific review, approved and rejected are distinct states. Authorization expiry, deleted/invalidated evidence or lost connections require the original review/retry controls. A network request is not proof of receipt or approval. Reviewers use original verified received evidence, issued requirements, source limitations, audit history and server decisions; they cannot promote local success optimistically.

## Live sharing and privacy

Connecting, claiming, recording, collecting science and submitting evidence do not enable live sharing. Authorize the separate live scope in the browser, then explicitly consent for this flight. Review the exact organization/server, active-member audience, selected public label, included fields and expiry. The server can disable monitoring. Consent lasts at most eight hours and cannot outlive the browser grant. Restarting does not restore sharing.

The live map shows only permitted current state. Duration is calculated at the original source update and does not prove recording continuity or collection time. Stale positions expire/clear; no new shared track history or replay is stored. Stop Sharing removes current visibility without ending recording or deleting research. Shared card status is local status, not received or approved science.

## Roles, reviews and notifications

Supported roles are Administrator, Manager, Reviewer and Pilot. Administrators manage the organization; managers manage programs; reviewers need appropriate campaign access; pilots contribute research. Read-only desktop access is a permission scope, not an additional server role. Membership, wrong-server identity, expiry, revocation, revisions and permissions are checked on the server regardless of which controls are visible.

Authorized administrators can find the existing notification settings through portal task navigation. Discord remains optional/OFF by default. It supports separately opted-in flight-sharing events, independently reviewer-approved research, and accepted documented exceptions. Pilot flight announcements require additional explicit consent. Approved research/exception announcements require exact external disclosure. Internal organization events keep their original role/privacy rules. No original science files, precise telemetry, credentials or private review notes are added to Discord. Reconnects/retries do not authorize duplicate announcements; uncertain acknowledgments remain held.

## Source permissions, storage and deletion

Opening a source display or help does not authorize a download. Use the Scientific Library's original permission, storage, quota and source-selection controls. Source-owned deletion removes dependent products under the existing safeguards. Local deletion cannot recall an organization copy already expressly transmitted and received. Disconnecting an organization does not delete personal research.
