# AeroScience v0.7.0 — Future Science Suite & Mission Architecture

**Owner direction recorded October 23, 2026. Status: future-capability architecture research, not approved v0.7.0 instrument implementation.** Maintain this document alongside [technical research](v0.7.0-technical-research.md), [design decisions](v0.7.0-product-decisions.md), and the independent [v0.6.0 scope](https://github.com/JustSeanC/AeroScience/blob/develop/v0.6.0/docs/roadmap/v0.6.0-scope.md). No feature development, merge or release authorized.

## Owner's goal

AeroScience should eventually offer far more than basic atmospheric data and existing geographic remote sensing: **airborne imaging, optical/thermal/multispectral/hyperspectral collection, weather probing, modeled dropsondes, hurricane hunting and severe-weather studies**, and other future scientific mission classes. The optional research-organization and fictional hardware/flight-test system of v0.7.0 must be compatible with those extensions without constructing them all now. Pilot workflow stays local-first with explicit postflight organization submission. Future live Mission Control remains deferred.

## Verified inherited code and current limits (develop/v0.6.0, 2026-10-23)

- `src/AeroScience.Missions/ScienceCatalog.cs`: **eight statically registered instrument definitions** (four simulator atmospheric channels: temperature, pressure, wind, density; thermal/multispectral/hyperspectral external-data imagers; DEM-derived terrain scanner) and **six mission templates**: General Research, Vertical Profile, Geographic Survey, Observation Flight, Cloud Structure Survey, Atmospheric Transect.
- `src/AeroScience.Shared/ResearchModels.cs`: `MissionTemplateKind` is a fixed enum; `InstrumentConfiguration`, `MeasurementReading`, `ScienceObservation` and `ScienceProvenance` distinguish acquisition and source. Existing Universal and Realistic modes should not be replaced or multiplied.
- `src/AeroScience.Missions/ScientificProviders.cs`: real referenced satellite/terrain assets via source-provider adapters, with source dates and numeric bands. **Existing 'imagers' do not take new aircraft camera photographs**; imagery and numeric pixels are from external sources at their original dates.
- MSFS 2024 SDK lists atmospheric/cloud/precipitation/visibility variables, but *listing a SimVar is not proof* that an atmospheric field exists at arbitrary off-aircraft positions or that real 3-D hurricanes, dropsondes, onboard Doppler radar, source calibrated optical pixels, or native camera-frame products are supplied. Source: https://docs.flightsimulator.com/msfs2024/retail/programming-apis/simvars/simulation-variables/
- NOAA operational examples: https://www.omao.noaa.gov/aircraft-operations/noaa-hurricane-hunters ; https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/observational-instruments/ . Dropsondes sample independent descending positions and vertical profiles; aircraft-level local ambient readings alone cannot recreate them.

## Architectural seams to reserve in v0.7.0

### A. Mission purpose is separate from sensing and processing

Maintain a universal `MissionPlan` and independent `ScienceCollectionSession`. A future `MissionProfile/ObjectiveKind` descriptor can identify e.g. SurfaceImaging, AtmosphericSounding, StormReconnaissance, CloudPhysics, EnvironmentalTransect or FlightTest, but **do not add a new mutually exclusive global operating mode**. A mission may include multiple goals, cards, instruments and patterns in one flight. Cards ask for evidence capabilities (e.g. `temperature_profile`, `raster_frame`, `wind_vector_samples`) rather than being hardwired to current instrument IDs or `MissionTemplateKind` enum members. Future mission templates can be registered through versioned metadata when actually implemented; keep current saved plans readable.

### B. Capability declarations and source adapters

Reserve typed, versioned read-only capability metadata for an instrument/sensor:

- Stable `instrumentId` and `modelRevision`; scientific output channels; units, valid range, sampling cadence and uncertainty/model assumptions.
- Sources: **SimReporter** (actual simulator observation at aircraft), **DocumentedModel** (derived/fictitious experimental output), **HistoricalExternalDataset** (original scene/date), **ImportedObservation**, and **HumanObservation**. Maintain existing serialized enums compatibly; proposed source categories may be additive descriptors rather than replacing current enum values.
- Sample geometry: aircraft point, explicit sensor footprint/raster/swath, line transect, vertical probe trajectory, and spatial volume/voxel if someday supported.
- Time: simulator flight UTC/sample time vs instrument observation/acquisition time vs external original scene date vs processing timestamp. Validity, gap and calibration/source revision must accompany outputs.
- Acquisition authority and support state: available, unavailable, simulated/model-only, or requires imported external data; unknown must not be represented as valid scientific measurement.

This is a versioned contract design; **do not add an unsafe arbitrary plugin loader or executable user sensor scripts** just for future-proofing. The confirmed v0.7.0 experimental-sensor creator uses validated configurable templates.

### C. Extendable scientific result/product family

Scientific evidence must not assume it is only a single numeric time series. Allow future product classes including:
1. numeric timed observations (existing atmospheric readings);
2. trajectory/profile series (aircraft vertical profiles or simulated dropsondes with per-sample geo-altitude-time);
3. georeferenced image frame or raster scene, with camera parameters/FOV/pose, footprint, radiometric status, quality and original pixel payload (if actually acquired);
4. 2D/3D scan, swath, grid or radar-style volume, only if backed by a real supported source or clearly labeled simulation;
5. event/annotation, deployment, calibration/model or pilot report.

Use separate storage/artifacts per product, stable IDs, source references, content hashes, units/datum, time, QA, limitations and linkable card evidence intervals. A test card declares which product/evidence capabilities it needs; evaluators cannot assume every sensor returns a scalar at 1 Hz.

### D. Special scientific workflows (future research only)

**Imaging:** Assess real MSFS screenshot/camera capture feasibility and permissions separately; if unsupported, allow clearly labeled simulated camera or externally dated imagery. Do not call satellite scene sampling "captured onboard imagery." Eventually support optical/sensor geometry, camera attitude, footprint calculations, overlaps, exposure metadata, georegistration and large storage streaming.

**Weather probing:** Support vertical profile and probe/deployment event contracts, release location/time, independent probe trajectory, fall physics, environmental field source, per-sample pressure/temperature/humidity/wind and QC. **Do not invent dropsonde humidity or winds at positions not accessible from the simulator or a documented model/dataset.** A flight-level reading at the aircraft is not a sonde observation.

**Hurricane hunting:** Later allow storm-relative moving center/reference frame, radial/figure-four/eyewall and environmental transect patterns, storm track date/time, multi-pass coordination, modeled dropsonde release, wind/pressure gradients, storm features and uncertainty. A real/historic NOAA observation or model dataset must be labeled with its actual dates; an MSFS storm is a simulated weather environment, not a real reconnaissance report. No assumption that simulator weather fields accurately model hurricane structure.

**Other future missions:** cloud microphysics, lidar/point clouds, atmospheric rivers, wildfire/smoke mapping, ocean/coastal work, pollution monitoring. Keep as candidate families, not v0.7.0 commitments.

### E. Data and deletion contracts inherited from v0.6.0 requirements

Science Library growth should get more efficient through source-aware reuse and deduplication. Reuse scientifically compatible locally retained terrain/imagery/source tiles if available; avoid arbitrary global catalog count limits (bounded pagination/job processing is acceptable). Large raster/volumetric products should be streamed/lazy-read with clear user-selected storage capacity and no silent uploads.

When a user permanently deletes external datasets or scientific collection data, remove the selected owned originals/derived caches without hidden safety copies, backups or archives. **Preserve already committed, independently stored mission observations/results only where their own saved artifacts actually contain enough data to render or reevaluate; otherwise clearly mark missing source dependencies.** Organization servers have distinct explicitly submitted copies/retention contracts; a local deletion must not be falsely represented as server deletion.

### F. Campaign/flight-test integration

One shared versioned campaign/test-card framework should evaluate scientific observations, simulated hardware channels, images, profiles, quality/coverage, deployment events, pilot reports and aircraft regime predicates. Multiple independently evaluated cards can reference one flight. Preserve exactly which data artifacts and model/source versions were used. Existing v0.7.0 local-only explicit evidence submission must allow pilot preview and size control; binary imaging products are optional separate consent, not automatically transmitted. Future live monitoring is another explicitly approved security/consent layer, **not delivered in v0.7.0**.

**Owner 2026-10-25 NASA-alignment refinement:** Five concrete science/mission UX requirements are now approved for v0.7.0: research-question-centered campaign/card objectives; integrated *local* mission operations view; map-layer provenance/coverage/measurement explanation; synchronized mission science/test timeline; and campaign-level compatible/approved scientific coverage and gaps. These must reuse existing scientific pipelines and remain separate from new v0.9.0 instrument families. See `v0.7.0-product-decisions.md` and `v0.7.0-reuse-gap-matrix.md` for requirements/acceptance. Full opt-in **remote** MTS-style live Mission Control might belong in v1.0 or later, with **no release commitment**; not v0.7, v0.8, or v0.9 scope by implication.

## Recommended release boundary

**v0.7.0 (approved goals/design only):** organizational campaigns, versioned evidence/test cards; configurable experimental-sensor templates; typed capabilities / future-ready evidence product references only to the extent necessary; no assumption that all evidence is a scalar, a remote satellite scene, or a specific aircraft. Migration and compatibility tests for older instrument definitions and flight archives.

**v0.9.0 — owner-selected planned science scope:** weather reconnaissance, simulated dropsondes/atmospheric probes, temperature/pressure/humidity/wind profiles, vertical sampling, turbulence/atmospheric structure; hurricane reconnaissance, storm-relative patterns, eyewall observation, simulated sonde release, storm structure/intensity and coordinated multi-aircraft research; simulated aerial cameras and photographic surveys; wildfire, ocean, environmental and airborne-radar research. See [v0.9.0 planned scope](v0.9.0-scope.md) for all items, source/model feasibility dependencies and phased acceptance. **This is a release target, not a scientific fidelity guarantee, implementation start or completed feasibility review.** Live organization monitoring is *not* part of this authorization and remains separately deferred. v0.7.0 only reserves compatibility seams and acceptance fixtures.

## Required architecture checks before v0.7.0 freezes

1. A simple existing atmospheric research mission and its older archives still load and evaluate unchanged.
2. A future profile-like synthetic **fixture** (not a falsely functioning dropsonde) can be referenced by a test-card evidence capability without adding a hardcoded card type or a new operating mode.
3. A future raster-like evidence **manifest fixture** with acquisition/original dataset dates, spatial coverage, quality and stable source ID can be represented without uploading the original binary automatically.
4. A fictional experimental sensor can be configured and evaluated with documented source/model provenance; missing variables produce insufficient evidence, not invented simulated observations.
5. A mixed science + aircraft test campaign and several cards share one recorded sortie without duplicate flight storage.
6. User-selected external dataset deletion and scientific collection deletion cannot be defeated by a hidden cache/archive/backup; missing dependencies display honestly.
7. No live telemetry or automatic organizational scientific uploads appear just because a capability was declared.

**Scope note:** Researching and planning extensibility must not delay urgent v0.6.0 native EMIT/source catalog improvements or be used as a pretext for an unfinished, unbounded plugin architecture in v0.7.0.

## Owner roadmap update — targeted v0.9.0 science suites (2026-10-23)

All specifically requested future mission families are now in the owner-directed [v0.9.0 expanded science scope](v0.9.0-scope.md). Design v0.7.0 to support dynamic instrument/product capabilities, independent moving-probe trajectories, image/raster assets, storm-relative reference frames, environmental grids and eventual radar-style datasets **without implementing those instruments or missions in v0.7.0**. Multi-aircraft v0.9 research must be possible by planned assignments and explicit postflight submissions; real-time multi-aircraft telemetry remains unapproved.

The v0.7.0 definition/manifest fixtures and backwards compatibility gates are mandatory architectural preparation; do not enlarge the v0.7.0 product scope into operational hurricane probes, image capture, radar, fire/ocean models or novel weather simulation. Existing heritage science sources must preserve dates, local dedupe/reuse, deletion rules, and simulated-vs-reported-vs-historical provenance. The v0.9.0 scope will be refined by later feasibility and scientific acceptance studies.
