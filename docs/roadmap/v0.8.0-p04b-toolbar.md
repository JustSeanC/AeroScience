Branch: **develop/v0.8.0**. P04b began only after P04a passed its focused checks and was committed as **34825b2e0b9d1de44995c638f1a07a0fd7c6a424**. The [P04 handoff](v0.8.0-p04-handoff.md) records exact implementation/integration SHAs and completed CI receipts.

## Organization and original controls

The native panel now has three principal sections: **Flight**, **Science**, and **Cards** when the existing flight-bound research projection identifies relevant cards. The persistent header exposes **Map** with an accessible “Map, route & waypoints” name. Map remains the original contextual view, retaining follow/free pan, fit/zoom, actual route and bounded track, saved-plan selection/paging, waypoint inspection and next observed target. No navigation command controls the aircraft.

Flight retains actual mission/aircraft identity, recording, telemetry, duration, original recording mode/start/finish and pilot observations. Original saved-plan policy and readiness/permission checks still govern recording; the UI does not replace their startup policy. Science presents the confirmed phase/session, authorized Start/Pause/Resume/End/Rearm buttons directly, a single nonterminal primary action, retained counts, trigger/hold state and acquisition diagnostics. Disabled instruments retain their original controls in a named disclosure; enabled instruments appear first. Interval drafts, Apply/Use confirmed, acknowledgements and every original calibration/source escape remain intact. Detailed readings, grade bounds and evidence remain specialist disclosures. Important source/acquisition messages stay visible. The legacy instrument acquisition aggregate is explicitly labeled separately from the authoritative collection phase.

Cards retains the existing controller's focused card, independent activation, multiple cards, compatibility restrictions, test points, manual/automatic attempts and supported next-step/detection controls. Focusing or changing sections sends no activation or completion command. Known relevant cards remain contextual during stale state, with original actions disabled; a confirmed empty/no-flight context returns to Flight. The dashboard remains responsible for detailed original evidence, evaluation/review/waiver, organization delivery and permission workflows.

Section switches move only presentation/focus/scroll. Flight and science remain independent in the persistent banner. Small panels scroll their content while the SDK title/identity/status/navigation remain visible; selecting a section reaches its actual content. Reopen preserves the selected pane and matching pressed state. Arrow/Home/End navigation and standard button keyboard behavior are supported. Native retains its readable dark palette in both light/dark browser-host review fixtures; Auto/Light/Dark dashboard preferences are unchanged.

## One state projection and bounded metadata

The native build generates **AeroScience.FlightStatus.js** from the dashboard's canonical `presentation-state.js` and `current-flight-state.js`, wrapping them as a classic script and compiling/parsing ES2017 independently. The native adapter normalizes existing mission-intelligence fields into this read-only projection. There is no second recorder, science coordinator, trigger evaluator, truth store or science model. Stale, paused, invalid-position, wrong mission/session and storage-hold evidence cannot assert current collection. A missing/incompatible presentation adapter yields unknown science and disabled actions, without reporting usable telemetry as a malformed engine reply.

Optional `ToolbarIntelligence.RetainedScience` reuses **MissionIntelligenceService.Retained**, the same short-lived indexed original/derived/product counts used by the dashboard. Diagnostic acquisition rows/sets and flight samples remain separate. Missing/old/null counts are unavailable, never zero inferred from telemetry. Under pressure this optional metadata can be omitted with DetailsOmitted; the UI says unavailable.

Protocol **1**, existing commands/capabilities, **1,024-byte commands / 8,000-byte replies**, source/profile and engine identities, mission/session/control revisions, exact request/receipt correlation, retry identity, late-response ordering and stale-state rejection remain intact. The optional reply field introduces no command or authority. Existing byte-reduction policy preserves original identifiers/receipts; the final count omission cannot truncate IDs or alter evidence. TemplateElement, dataStorage/import/bootstrap order, SDK native frame/title/drag/resize ownership and hidden-panel listener/timer cleanup are retained.

Catalog **v6**, **311 canonical English messages**, unchanged schema/manifest contract v1. New critical action labels, section labels, accessible Map name, counts and diagnostic summaries use the existing adapter/catalog. Older, unsupported or mismatched metadata retains English fallback. es-ES/fr-FR/de-DE/pt-BR remain **English preview resources**, not reviewed complete translations. Scientific values, units, source dates, IDs and proper names are not translated or changed.

## Integration corrections

Two genuine issues found by the broader browser run were repaired without weakening assertions:

1. Hiding the dashboard's idle Science area also hid the original mission-intelligence poller, preventing synchronization after an external/native recording start. The existing poller stays visible in Current Flight; no second subscription/controller was added.
2. Coincident/nearby UTC evidence events could obscure each other's touch targets. Existing timeline targets now stack/reflow with at least 44px targets, preserving original UTC, IDs, DOM order, filtering, paging, inspection and selection associations. A deterministic simultaneous-event regression checks every target, keyboard selection and reflow. No scientific evidence or timestamp was changed.
