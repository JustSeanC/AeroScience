A full-width cockpit presents the actual flight name, aircraft, valid elapsed time, connection/freshness, independent recording and collection status, confirmed session, enabled instruments and separate original/derived/external-product counts. The original science buttons move with the original Science Journey into the cockpit. Pending commands disable conflicting actions and do not update confirmed collection. The original recording form, operating mode, conservative arm option and readiness flow remain intact. End mission and End current science session retain their distinct handlers and availability.

Named shortcuts reach the existing map/route/waypoints, pilot observation editor, timeline/original evidence, instruments/intervals/calibration, Flight Details and full planner. Objective focus, multiple active cards, compatibility, automatic/manual attempts, evaluation, evidence and review/waiver behavior remain with their original controller. Sensor Details, Advanced Diagnostics, collection sessions, automation/events, grading limitations and provenance remain accessible. Critical source/calibration/geometry/storage warnings stay outside specialist disclosures.

Auto theme and the shared tokens govern the cockpit. Desktop and narrow layouts wrap controls and scientific identities; status is textual, keyboard focus survives polling and controls retain accessible names. No motion is required. The legacy Flight Details view receives its original live nodes back when Current Flight closes. Recorded review hides live controls and live scanner guidance, preserves exact archived flight identity and retains deletion invalidation.

## Scanner guidance and scientific boundary

`SensorOperatingProjection` adds optional read-only `ScannerSpacingGuidance` to an enabled Terrain Scanner's existing operating card. It requires a fresh matching mission/session/configuration and collecting phase. It calls the existing `SensorGeometry.ForInstrument` and `Geodesy.Distance`; no scientific rule, acquisition calculation, calibration, source selection or grade changes. Configured interval × actual groundspeed is **predicted** spacing; the existing AGL/orientation/profile model supplies along-track footprint extent. Missing or invalid intersection withholds an overlap conclusion. No maximum safe speed or uninterrupted-coverage guarantee is invented.

The view separately compares the last two supported Terrain Scanner product centers and **original flight-packet times**, limited to the existing 30-product read and matching mission/session/source. At least one valid source sample, distinct product/intent and valid coordinates/timestamps are required. This is straight-line saved-product spacing, not traveled distance, present-profile compliance, physical detector timing or measured coverage. It may include products processed later; packet and processing times are explicitly distinct. Insufficient metadata remains unavailable. Rejected acquisitions, missed intervals and source status are displayed separately. A shorter interval cannot guarantee overlap or successful acquisition.

The original live map/track and layer semantics remain. Expired, paused or invalid telemetry cannot retain a live aircraft symbol. Tracks, planned routes/areas, modeled extents and original observations remain distinct. No continuous corridor, DEM reconstruction or LiDAR point-cloud system is implemented; those remain P05.
