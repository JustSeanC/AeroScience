**Proposed decisions, not implemented features or acceptance.** These recommendations
allow incremental development without treating every open detail as a blocker. Scope
changes need review; no question here authorizes deleting evidence or weakening integrity.

## Research inputs to collect in feature PRs

- Manufacturer and official SDK/source references for every proposed parameter/rule,
  including conditions, revision date and unavailable fields. Existing references start
  in [instrument profiles](../v0.4.0-instrument-profiles.md), not fictional certificates.
- Provider pagination/scene/asset/band/QA/size/ETag/range behavior and direct-transfer terms;
  catalogue metadata and numeric original data need separate verification.
- Real CMR/AVIRIS granule families and official user-auth/browser paths. Do not obtain
  shared credentials or promise support from product names alone.
- Representative survey/profile/transect mission obligations and expected pilot actions,
  grade-policy examples and resource measurements. Keep numbers proposed until measured.
- Exact installed Windows/MSFS/paired Safari/Quest test evidence on future candidate bytes.
  Development-container tests cannot resolve those acceptance entries.

No external credential or hardware access is needed to complete this documentation PR.
None of the above uncertainties authorizes claiming a feature complete before its evidence.
