# AeroScience v0.7.0 — Readiness

**Development checkpoint: P02 in-flight Personal Research operations. Not release-ready.**

Baseline: published stable v0.6.0, `9dd2d8cda7ddb6d73414808f75010726871205c2`. Its source, tag, release assets and historical scientific evidence remain unchanged. Development identity is 0.7.0/prerelease, with a separate v0.7.0 validation namespace. The README's existing stable v0.6.0 / development v0.7.0 structure is preserved.

Implemented: local My Research entry; one Flight-Regime Validation template; immutable card revisions; editable plans through the existing Mission Planner; frozen card references in recorded plan snapshots; IAS/MSL tolerance and sustained-hold assessment; saved Pass/Fail/Insufficient Evidence results; bounded inventory/result pages; offline reopening; UTC interval selection and original telemetry sequence/hash references. Results use existing flight data and the existing mission database; no raw recording copies, provider calls, accounts or organization uploads.

The existing mission grade rubric evaluates mission-design, operating, route and acquisition obligations. It does not express a card's sustained IAS/MSL hold. The small `FlightRegimeEvaluation` extension supplies this rule without altering mission grades, scientific computations, source acquisition, operating assessments or collection controls.

Mission schema 9 adds `personal_research_cards` and `personal_research_attempts`. The existing verified upgrade-backup policy remains in place. Migration does not rewrite old telemetry, plan JSON, measurements or grades. New plan associations are additive/null-omitted; historical snapshots retain their prior serialized form. Attempt references cascade when their flight is deleted; external-source deletion cannot remove the separately recorded flight evidence.

Assessment policy `flight-regime-1`: inclusive target tolerances, contiguous observed samples at intervals no greater than 1.5 seconds, no accumulated disjoint holds. Recorded pause/gap/connection edges break continuity, including subsecond simulator pauses skipped by the recorder. Results identify exact UTC boundaries, original sequence range/count, evidence digest, frozen plan digest and card revision. Processing streams a selected interval of at most 24 hours with cancellation; retained card/flight/result ownership has no count ceiling. Read snapshots do not hold a writer lock during evaluation; publication is a short transaction with idempotent request identity.

P02 adds card selection inside the existing Mission Planner, independent Activate/Deactivate and Begin/End Attempt controls in Mission Control and the native toolbar, and a recorded card/flight/science evidence timeline in Mission History and My Research. Compatible cards can be active together: both inclusive IAS and MSL target intervals must intersect for every active pair. Conflicting targets require deactivation first. Up to 16 cards may be selected for one flight (four per native reply); retained cards, flights and attempts have no overall ownership ceiling. No card activates automatically.

Mission schema 10 adds frozen per-flight definitions, card-state revision and marked-attempt tables. Commands reuse the coordinator, mission events and durable collection receipt table. Exact flight/card/attempt identity and expected revision prevent stale or duplicate commands from retargeting an attempt. Browser session/CSRF and native protocol protections remain in force. Reads and timeline pages are bounded; evaluation streams existing raw samples after flight end. Flight termination and process recovery interrupt open attempts and deactivate cards. Interrupted attempts remain Insufficient Evidence even if some samples would otherwise meet a hold. No card command changes flight recording or scientific collection.

Only IAS and MSL altitude are supported. Mach, AGL, general criterion authoring, organization assignments, uploads and hosting remain unimplemented. v0.9.0 science plans remain documentation only. FAA chart rendering stays deferred; experimental EMIT's genuine-original gate remains pending.

Automated and CI results are tracked in the [development tracker](roadmap/v0.7.0-development-tracker.md). New v0.7.0 provider, ENVI and workspace release records explicitly say pending. They do not copy or relabel v0.6.0 acceptance. No installer, public release, tag or owner acceptance is created by this checkpoint.
