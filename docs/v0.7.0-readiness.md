# AeroScience v0.7.0 — Readiness

**Development checkpoint: P06 Atmospheric Transect Research. Not release-ready.**

Baseline: published stable v0.6.0, `9dd2d8cda7ddb6d73414808f75010726871205c2`. Its source, tag, release assets and historical scientific evidence remain unchanged. Development identity is 0.7.0/prerelease, with a separate v0.7.0 validation namespace. The README's existing stable v0.6.0 / development v0.7.0 structure is preserved.

Implemented: local My Research entry; one Flight-Regime Validation template; immutable card revisions; editable plans through the existing Mission Planner; frozen card references in recorded plan snapshots; IAS/MSL tolerance and sustained-hold assessment; saved Pass/Fail/Insufficient Evidence results; bounded inventory/result pages; offline reopening; UTC interval selection and original telemetry sequence/hash references. Results use existing flight data and the existing mission database; no raw recording copies, provider calls, accounts or organization uploads.

The existing mission grade rubric evaluates mission-design, operating, route and acquisition obligations. It does not express a card's sustained IAS/MSL hold. The small `FlightRegimeEvaluation` extension supplies this rule without altering mission grades, scientific computations, source acquisition, operating assessments or collection controls.

Mission schema 9 adds `personal_research_cards` and `personal_research_attempts`. The existing verified upgrade-backup policy remains in place. Migration does not rewrite old telemetry, plan JSON, measurements or grades. New plan associations are additive/null-omitted; historical snapshots retain their prior serialized form. Attempt references cascade when their flight is deleted; external-source deletion cannot remove the separately recorded flight evidence.

Assessment policy `flight-regime-1`: inclusive target tolerances, contiguous observed samples at intervals no greater than 1.5 seconds, no accumulated disjoint holds. Recorded pause/gap/connection edges break continuity, including subsecond simulator pauses skipped by the recorder. Results identify exact UTC boundaries, original sequence range/count, evidence digest, frozen plan digest and card revision. Processing streams a selected interval of at most 24 hours with cancellation; retained card/flight/result ownership has no count ceiling. Read snapshots do not hold a writer lock during evaluation; publication is a short transaction with idempotent request identity.

P02 adds card selection inside the existing Mission Planner, independent Activate/Deactivate and Begin/End Attempt controls in Mission Control and the native toolbar, and a recorded card/flight/science evidence timeline in Mission History and My Research. Compatible cards can be active together: both inclusive IAS and MSL target intervals must intersect for every active pair. Conflicting targets require deactivation first. Up to 16 cards may be selected for one flight (four per native reply); retained cards, flights and attempts have no overall ownership ceiling. No card activates automatically.

Mission schema 10 adds frozen per-flight definitions, card-state revision and marked-attempt tables. Commands reuse the coordinator, mission events and durable collection receipt table. Exact flight/card/attempt identity and expected revision prevent stale or duplicate commands from retargeting an attempt. Browser session/CSRF and native protocol protections remain in force. Reads and timeline pages are bounded; evaluation streams existing raw samples after flight end. Flight termination and process recovery interrupt open attempts and deactivate cards. Interrupted attempts remain Insufficient Evidence even if some samples would otherwise meet a hold. No card command changes flight recording or scientific collection.

P03 adds **My Research → Overview & Compare**: full-history counts, latest saved outcomes, current-revision saved-Pass/demo status, bounded attempt history and explicit cross-flight comparison. One marked attempt stays one attempt after re-evaluation; identical postflight flight/revision/UTC intervals also group their evaluations. Both kinds remain clearly distinguished and may overlap. Counts span revisions and are not independent-trial statistics. Interrupted or unevaluated activity is never treated as a failed test. All original evaluation records remain available in the collapsed Saved Local Results archive.

Comparisons retain each card revision, requirements, flight/date, outcome and explanation. They stream original evidence to verify the retained sample/pause/plan digest, show actual bounded sample/gap previews and open the existing flight timeline, replay and scientific results. The shared Chart.js/table components display required versus verified recorded holds. Insufficient, unevaluated or mismatched evidence has no achieved-hold bar. No averages, success rates, trend claims or copied recordings. Bounded pages and a per-request 2–8 comparison limit do not cap retained history. Schema remains 10 with one additive read index, no new tables or storage service.

P04 adds **My Research → Choose an Experiment → Fly Now / Customize** for Flight-Regime Validation. Fly Now prepares/selects a local plan and guides the pilot through the existing Mission Control readiness and recording controls. It never starts a flight or changes aircraft settings. Disconnected pilots can prepare and review targets; normal recording requirements remain enforced. Customize opens an independent editable card/plan in the existing full Mission Planner, including its routes, patterns, instruments and card selection. Target edits create a new revision of the copy; historical cards and flight snapshots remain unchanged.

Preparation uses a versioned descriptor and one atomic operation in the existing mission database. Exact request identities survive browser reload/retry, avoid duplicate cards/plans and preserve later draft/save decisions. Unmodified default flights reuse a retained experiment for cross-flight comparison. Schema stays 10; no new database, dependency, recorder, downloader or template engine. The starter's 120 ± 5 knots IAS / 3,000 ± 100 feet MSL / 30-second examples require pilot review and do not assert aircraft safety. A route, external data and additional instruments are optional for this telemetry-based card; scientific collection keeps its separate controls and requirements.

P05 adds **Vertical Atmospheric Profile** as the second Personal Research starter. It reuses the existing Vertical Profile mission and simulator atmospheric instruments. Its distinct version-2 card captures the scientific question and flexible neighboring MSL-band collection requirements, rather than IAS/altitude hold targets. Default collection requires three adjacent 500-foot bands with three consecutive valid temperature readings per band; pilots can customize this approach for achievable altitudes anywhere. Pressure, wind and density are optional, original-unit simulator observations. A climb without valid collected science cannot complete the investigation.

Atmospheric results use retained scientific observations, not raw flight packets to backfill a stopped/paused session. Collection-complete, partial and insufficient outcomes describe evidence coverage, not a proven hypothesis. Existing research storage/history/comparison retains exact revisions, observation/event hashes, Library identity and the frozen plan. Explicit evidence verification precedes plots; bounded actual-point graphs and shared paged tables preserve original numeric values, quality, UTC and source. Reading inspection opens the existing science timeline; compatible profiles compare across flights and reopen offline. Permanently deleting collected science removes these dependent summaries and prevents reconstruction from raw flight context. Flight-Regime evaluation remains separate and unchanged.

No new database/table or schema-version increase: mission schema 10/science schema 4 gain an additive observation-time read index. Existing native controls render atmospheric criteria and page one full definition within the same byte guard; Flight-Regime-only paging remains four cards.

P06 adds **Atmospheric Transect** as the third starter, using the existing mission template and atmospheric instruments. A distinct version-3 card records geographic sampling goals rather than aircraft targets or altitude bands. Defaults require three sampled locations at least 500 m apart, each with three consecutive valid temperature readings, and 1,000 m extent in one supported leg. WGS84 distances/indexed station reuse handle worldwide and dateline positions; revisits cannot create new locations, and broken legs cannot combine into a completed collection. Original scientific observations, quality, units, positions, UTC, session and source references remain authoritative. Missing science is never reconstructed from raw flight context.

The optional/null-omitted saved-plan `StartScienceArmed` preference reuses the existing coordinator's separate science start. Transect preparations record the flight first and wait for **Start new science session**. **Wait for me to start science collection** is editable in the full Mission Planner. This does not enable geographic automation or change old plan JSON, P05 defaults, scientific calculations, existing automation guards or schema versions.

Transect reports show actual sample points/path links, temperature versus supported distance, optional pressure/wind/density, sampling locations/extent/gaps, altitude/time/source limitations and exact observations with timeline inspection. The existing Leaflet/Chart.js/table components and research comparison are reused. Maps/plots create no observations between samples; latitude beyond Mercator's display range remains available numerically. Preview/query/work bounds limit individual analyses, never Library ownership. Frozen planned configuration and source/session context remain visible; weather, altitude, time and settings can confound geographic comparisons. Offline restart, mixed native cards and permanent-science-deletion protections are covered by focused tests; completed full milestone results are in the tracker.
