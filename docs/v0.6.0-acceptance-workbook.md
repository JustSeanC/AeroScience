Original chart retention, NASR points/runways, Class B/C/D/E and supported literal SAA boundaries are
implemented with independent genuine-original checks. TFR horizontal boundaries/notice joins and
cautious screening are implemented; complete NOTAM segment/time/altitude/activation acceptance remains
pending. Chart rendering/neatlines and the production EMIT reader remain **Implementation Pending**.
No owner/device scenario above is pre-marked passed by these cloud results.

For Group E in a private installed build: import the exact Class/SAA edition and inspect two regions,
holes and altitude Unknowns; verify category/opacity/list controls. Explicitly refresh TFR boundaries,
compare an actual notice against the FAA site, inspect notices without geometry and confirm the amber
Unknown legend. Screen both intersecting and nonintersecting saved routes: no result may clear a mission
while time, altitude or coverage is unknown. Disable network and reopen retained data; reconnect a
missing aviation drive; check keyboard/focus and physical iPad touch separately. Do not claim active,
future or expired restrictions without independently proven UTC/altitude/segment evidence.

Group E continuation (owner **Not Tested**): reimport an already verified SAA original; compare the
old/new interpretation IDs, retained literal boundaries and new full-circle details, units and
interpolation caveat. Verify offline views after restart, no duplicate transfer, old interpretation
availability and original hash preservation. Confirm unsupported partial/composite geometry remains
reported and no activation is inferred. Test schema-4 backup/recovery on copied disposable data.
