  opacity/visibility independently, and inspect the exact official notice. Verify legend text and

  the revision and reassess. No older answer or mixed source revision may appear current.

  recover when allowed. Opening/panning/review must never silently retrieve operational packets.

  coordinates must be unchanged and the original placement mode must resume.
- Confirm X/Escape, focus recovery, touch targets, 200% zoom and narrow iPad Safari. Verify original
  draft values and Mission/Science visibility return unchanged. Start recording and science after
  dismissing the advisory; no warning may gate simulation or collection.
