# Scientific comparison

Choose two actual acquisitions in Compare. Maps share position and zoom. Source dates,
flight dates, native grids and quality remain visible in Advanced evidence.

Numeric differences require matching measurement units, source kind, native resolution,
CRS, pixel orientation, archived sensor configuration and vertical datum. Each common
valid native pixel is counted once per acquisition. Missing or masked pixels are excluded.
Unknown source dates and incompatible quantities do not receive a numeric delta.

Differences are descriptive. Repeated simulator flights sampling one historical scene
are not independent observations of Earth change. Different satellite dates are not
simultaneous airborne measurements and do not establish causation.

Repeated straight-transect analysis uses actual endpoint proximity, intermediate
positions, direction, cross-track tolerance and packet continuity. Pilot turnarounds
are outside the scientific pass. A planned pass alone is never counted as completed.
