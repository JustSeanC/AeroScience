# Science Workspace

Open **Science Workspace** in the sidebar. Choose a mission, several saved missions,
or All Missions. Science Map shows saved QA-valid native pixel support. Transparent
areas contain no displayed valid values; acquisition rectangles do not establish
continuous survey coverage. Use Load More for bounded archive browsing.

## Map

Choose a measurement, filter flight dates and inspect a colored pixel. Source dates
are independent of flight time. Fit Mission Track shows the saved plan and actual
recorded track. Build Native Composite uses the verified v0.4.0 native-grid engine;
GeoTIFF downloads preserve numeric values, resolution, CRS and nodata.

## Research Analysis

Review one mission and its instruments. Measurement values and acquisition timestamps
are counted separately. Wind speed and direction can share a timestamp. Charts use
actual archived packets and break at missing telemetry. No causal finding is inferred.

## Results and exports

Export the research ZIP for flight, science, events, original-source references and
hashes, and derived geographic products. Scientific review state is included; results
remain provisional while eligible post-flight work is pending. Replay is immediately
available for a completed flight without requiring Internet or the simulator.

## Sources

Scientific Data Library groups reusable sources by provider and collection, with
individual geographic regions and immutable provenance inside. A historical source
is reusable by multiple missions; it is not a new aircraft measurement.
