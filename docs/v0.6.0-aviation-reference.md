# Aviation maps and FAA references — v0.6.0 development

Development supports approved FAA original retention, offline airport/runway/navigation references,
Class B/C/D/E and supported SAA geometry, and conservative TFR boundaries/screening. Complete chart
rendering is **deferred by owner**. FAA catalogs, official approved downloads, metadata, hashes and
offline originals remain available. The ordinary interior viewer is disabled; normal installation
does not include chart-specific native dependencies. FAA/TFR/science layers remain independent.
Native EMIT application integration remains experimental; genuine-original acceptance is pending. Use the
[readiness report](v0.6.0-readiness.md) for exact validation status. Published v0.5.0 and v0.4.2 are preserved.

## Review a mission for published TFRs

From **Mission Planner → a saved mission → Review TFRs**, or **Overlays & Legend → TFR
Boundaries & Screening**, choose the saved route or a saved survey area. This is an advisory
review of the saved revision; unsaved edits are preserved and are not included until saved.
The same map moves into the review temporarily. Cyan geometry is the reviewed mission;
other mission/science layers return to their previous visibility when the review closes.

1. Choose **Check Saved Mission Against Retained TFRs**. Opening, panning, selecting and
   screening use retained host data; they do not download FAA XML/AIXM packets.
2. Read the summary: mission/geometry, time basis, saved MSL envelope, source age, returned
   areas, potentially intersecting notices, insufficient dimensions and assessed exclusions.
   A whole-mission time/altitude envelope does not predict individual waypoint arrivals.
3. Choose **Locate NOTAM … on map** or select a published boundary, then **Go to selected
   notice in list**. The whole source polygon is highlighted, never an invented 3D volume.
   TFR visibility/opacity are independent; the text legend explains every line style.
4. Use **Inspect NOTAM …** for published dates, limits and missing evidence, then the exact
   **official NOTAM** link. **Retrieve Official Operational Details** deliberately requests
   only that selected notice's bounded packets, subject to FAA consent, network policy and
   throttling. Advanced source identities, hashes and interpretation limits remain available.
5. To assess time, explicitly choose **Real-world planned UTC**. The date/time fields are UTC
   readings even if your device uses another timezone; the local display is explanatory only.
   Both times blank means time unassessed. Simulator time is never substituted for FAA UTC.
   MSL, AGL and standard-pressure/flight-level limits remain distinct; unsupported conversions
   stay Unknown. Original source units are retained alongside compatible display preferences.
6. Changing time, geometry, mission or source evidence invalidates older findings. Use
   **Reload saved mission** after a saved-plan revision changes. Reassess before relying on
   earlier results. Slow replies cannot overwrite a newer selection or mix source packets.
7. Review **Notices without usable geometry** too: they cannot be excluded geographically.
   Stale/offline data shows original success/attempt dates and remains explicitly non-current.
   Use **Refresh Official TFR Boundaries** only when you want a consented official update.

**Potential intersection** means published geography overlaps but operational/legal dimensions
remain unverified. **Outside assessed dimensions** applies only to supported time or altitude
checks for that area. **Unable to determine** and no plotted intersection never mean “Clear”
or “Safe to Fly”. Cancellation, recurring activation, aircraft applicability and complete
coverage remain unverified where unsupported. These are simulation references, never real-world
briefing or clearance advice. All mission recording and science controls remain available.

Close with X/Escape to return keyboard focus to the opener. Closing does not cancel downloads,
stop recording, change a saved mission or discard its editor draft. Installed Windows/physical
iPad/Quest acceptance remains separate from browser emulation and recorded-FAA-fixture tests.

## Deferred chart research (historical instructions)

The viewer steps and runtime notes below preserve existing research; they are **not available in
the ordinary 0.6.0 interface**. Use FAA Downloads to retain/review originals. No further interior clips,
neatline research or automatic native chart builds are part of the revised 0.6.0 work.

1. Use **FAA Catalog & Notices → FAA Downloads** to review and explicitly approve an exact original.
2. On the map, open **Overlays & Legend → Retained FAA Charts**. Choose the exact sheet and edition.
3. Inspect limitations, original hash, edition/currency, Unknown validity fields and projection
   details, then **Show**. Chart opacity and visibility are independent of mission, science,
   reference and TFR layers. **Hide**, X and Escape preserve recordings and retained originals.
4. Reopen offline with the same verified runtime and aviation drive. No FAA request is made by map
   inventory/tile reads. Closing the dashboard does not cancel approved original downloads.

Initial source-specific interiors cover the **2026-09-03** Hawaiian Islands Sectional, Boston TAC,
Boston Helicopter, IFR L01 and IFR H01 originals. These conservative pixel clips were reviewed against
the actual raster bodies; they are **not publisher neatlines**. Full chart edges, separate insets and
other rasters/editions remain explicitly unreviewed; no enclosing catalog rectangle is used as a
neatline. Independent source-derived color/CRS references cover all five families. Actual application
Windows rendering/packaging results and remaining printed-control/edge acceptance are recorded in the
tracker; source-reference generation alone is not a completed native test.

Charts are cartographic colors, not scientific samples. NAD83/WGS84 display is approximate and is not
certified navigation. Edition labels do not establish exact UTC validity: absent publisher fields stay
Unknown; a newer advertised label warns about an available edition without asserting it is in force.
The viewer never silently substitutes that edition. New chart initialization yields to an active
flight; warmed charts use a bounded serialized reader. Tiles are 256 pixels, native cache is 32 MiB,
warp scratch target is 16 MiB and at most sixteen tile requests may wait. An eight-second per-tile
deadline and original-file locks protect work; these bounds do not establish long-flight/device acceptance.
Derived tiles use at most 128 MiB plus small checksums in aviation storage, separately from approved
original-download allowances. Low disk prevents new cache writes; existing originals are preserved.

The Windows private builder accepts an optional **verified source-built** chart runtime. Pilots do not
install GIS/Python tools. Missing or changed runtime/data, an unreviewed sheet or a disconnected drive
produces an explicit unavailable state. Public release/SDK/runtime gates are unchanged.

## Chart runtime provenance and security

The reduced source-built graph uses GDAL 3.12.4, PROJ 9.8.1, libgeotiff 1.7.4, TIFF 4.7.2, zlib 1.3.2,
liblzma 5.8.3, curl 8.22.0, JSON-C 0.19-20260627, SQLite 3.53.4, static libjpeg-turbo 3.2.0 and header-only
nlohmann JSON 3.12.0. All component notices, actual `proj.db` and GDAL resources are part of a hashed
bounded manifest. Host-only vcpkg build tools, GPL LZMA utilities, proprietary drivers and the earlier
generic GDAL package are not distributed. Preserve bundled notices under `ChartRuntime/licenses`.
Public redistribution approval remains separate from private source/compile/loading verification.

OSV-2026-1068 is retained as a component advisory, not claimed fixed upstream. Its reported path is
`tj3Compress8 → jpeg_abort`. The supported source build disables TurboJPEG, statically links only the
libjpeg API into TIFF and now rejects any exported `tj*` API in every owned DLL. Actual new Windows export
results must pass before treating that guard as validated. Application input is read-only GTiff with
exact source hashes; output is fixed MEM/RGBA, with no JPEG encoding operation or user-supplied GDAL
options. No arbitrary URL/path API exists. Plugins, PAM/sidecars and PROJ network discovery are disabled;
DLL/data bytes are checked and held read-only. These controls mitigate the reported reachable surface;
they are not a blanket claim that this upstream version is vulnerability-free. Source/codec updates
require new import/export, original-color/coordinate and packaging checks.

## Choose map context and overlays

Every dashboard and scientific map has one **Basemap** selector and **Overlays & Legend**.
Choose offline geography, consented OSM streets, or your configured Esri layer. Outdoor provides
topographic context; Navigation provides road context, not certified aviation navigation.
Both use documented Esri static raster services and require your own public API key, provider
scopes/terms and account quotas. Configure it on the Windows companion in Settings → Maps & Basemaps.
Keys remain protected with Windows DPAPI. A missing key, incompatible raster scheme, quota or provider
failure leaves offline geography usable. Tiles are not bulk-downloaded for offline use.

Mission and Science visibility are independent. Hiding a route, science footprint, raster view or
observation does not change saved plans, collection, measurements, filters or grade evidence. Scientific
measurement/quality controls remain in their existing panel. The shared layer manager includes Library retained/catalog/selected/job areas, analysis native cells, comparison values/differences, coverage and retained imagery. Hiding a layer persists while its source is refreshed; it does not change the acquisition or analysis. A modeled footprint is not valid-pixel
coverage. Overlay choices are retained in this browser; paired devices keep their own view choices.
Close the overlay dialog with its top-right X or Escape; keyboard focus returns to its opener.

## Browse FAA catalogs and notices

Open **FAA Catalog & Notices** from Maps & Basemaps or Overlays & Legend. On the companion PC,
review the disclosure and explicitly allow FAA metadata retrieval. This consent is separate from
OSM/Esri maps and scientific-data consent. A paired device can read the host's records and request a
refresh after the host has enabled access; it cannot grant that access itself.

Choose VFR/TAC/Helicopter, IFR Low/High, NASR APT/NAV/FIX, or TFR summaries, then **Refresh FAA References**.
The host requests only fixed official metadata endpoints, at most 4 MiB each (NASR index plus at most two advertised cycle pages), with a five-minute per-category
manual refresh guard. This interval is application policy, not a promised FAA service level.
Pages contain at most 50 records. No chart original downloads begin merely by opening this view.
**Download & Retain Original** opens a bounded preview. Choose transfer and expanded-storage allowances, review the exact edition and approve. **Manage FAA Downloads** displays real saved jobs, measured bytes, errors and pause/resume/cancel/retry. Closing the window does not cancel work. **Official FAA Original ZIP** remains an external browser link; browser transfers are not managed by AeroScience.

Catalog edition labels come from official FAA paths. Exact UTC effective/expiry time, original size,
CRS, neatline and geographic accuracy remain **Unknown / Not verified** until original inspection.
Catalog presence does not establish geographic coverage. Approved originals are retained with local SHA-256 and ZIP/companion integrity checks. Geographic rendering, neatlines and update installation remain later work.

TFR summaries retain the original NOTAM ID, publisher date text, type/facility/state and description.
The list does not supply validated polygons, altitude limits or exact UTC activation schedules.
No circles or other guessed boundaries are drawn. Follow the official site and find the displayed
NOTAM ID for details. **These records cannot establish that a route has no restrictions.** They do
not block recording, mission start or scientific collection. This is a flight-simulation reference,
not a real-world aviation safety/preflight product.

## TFR boundaries and saved-mission screening

In any supported map's **Overlays & Legend**, choose **TFR Boundaries & Screening**. Enable FAA
reference access on the companion PC, then explicitly refresh the three bounded official metadata
sources. Reading or closing the panel never performs a provider request. Optional host monitoring
refreshes at most every five minutes subject to consent, network policy and provider backoff; paired
devices share the host snapshot. Last success, last attempt, offline/stale state and failures are separate.

Enable **FAA TFR boundaries · activation Unknown** independently of the basemap, routes and science.
Amber/dashed boundaries are official graphical geometry joined to matching notice metadata. They
are **not** confirmed-active restrictions: exact activation/cancellation, altitude and segment identity
remain Unknown. Review details and the official NOTAM. Notices with no usable boundary remain in an
accessible paged list and cannot be geographically filtered. Missing geometry never means no restriction.

Select a saved mission and either its route or one saved survey area to assess the retained horizontal
boundaries. The result is **Potential intersection** or **Unable to determine**, with source timestamp
and limitations. Even no plotted overlap cannot clear a route from this incomplete operational snapshot.
Real-world versus simulator time has not been inferred. Screening is advisory and changes no plan,
recording, collection, scientific values or grade. Zoom closer/use numeric bounds for a viewport up to
30 degrees per side; map pages return bounded geometry and can be continued in the list.

## Offline, stale and failed requests

Opening a catalog reads only retained metadata. The display distinguishes last success, last attempt,
failed refresh and offline cache. A failed or malformed response preserves the previous successful
snapshot. VFR/IFR records become stale after 24 hours, TFR summaries after 15 minutes; these are local
freshness policies, not authoritative restrictions validity. Disabled access preserves the cache.
A missing/changed aviation store is an error; AeroScience does not create a new empty replacement.
Aviation records have their own schema-5 store and do not become scientific sources or flight observations.

## Storage and recovery

Before first enabling FAA access, an optional new empty local aviation folder may be selected on the
companion PC. Existing data is never moved/deleted automatically. Physical free space, actual aviation
usage and reserved allowances are shown separately; an allowance is not a predicted download size.
Unknown sizes remain Unknown. A chart ZIP is acquired whole, even when only a small map region is needed.

The default policy requires a verified unmetered network; change it explicitly in FAA Downloads on the
companion PC if metered retrieval is intended. This does not change scientific or basemap consent.
Active flight recording takes priority: aviation transfers wait, and eligible jobs resume afterward.
Network failures retain approved work and provider retry guidance. Interrupted active work requires
explicit Resume / Retry. Safe byte-range resume is not established; restarting a ZIP still counts all
prior/uncertain bytes against its original approved allowance. A new larger scope requires fresh review.

Verified originals can be reopened and hash-checked offline. If an original changes, restore it; a duplicate
request will not silently overwrite it. Only owned unverified staging may be reset during retry. Safe
ZIP extraction does not establish cartographic accuracy or the validity of a chart's margins/insets.

## Import and display FAA NASR references

Select NASR in FAA Catalog & Notices, refresh, choose the exact current/next advertised APT, NAV or
FIX edition and review/approve its original ZIP. APT supplies airports/heliports and supported runway
endpoints/lines; NAV supplies NAVAIDs; FIX supplies navigation fixes. Class Airspace provides B/C/D/E
boundaries; SAA AIXM provides supported literal special-use boundaries. OurAirports worldwide search
remains separate from FAA references.

After the job is Completed, choose **Import FAA Reference Features** in FAA Downloads. This optional
local index uses additional aviation database space (size Unknown before import), creates no new
network download and preserves original files. Indexing yields to recording and insufficient physical
space; retry afterward. **Refresh FAA Jobs** reads actual stored state without starting a new transfer.

In Overlays & Legend, enable the desired **FAA** category and exact edition. Zoom 7 or closer; viewports
are bounded to 30 degrees per side and 250 drawn features/layer. **View … references** provides a
keyboard-accessible paged list with original coordinates and source details. Layers and opacity never
change mission/scientific data. Older advertised editions show a supersession warning in reference
review; cached/offline/stale timestamps cannot guarantee current operational status.

FAA specifies US NASR coordinates as NAD83; they are retained unchanged. Basemap display is approximate,
with no precise WGS84/epoch transformation or certified accuracy claim. Non-US/unknown-datum or
missing-coordinate records, and unsupported runway geometry, are omitted with counts. A runway line
connects two actual published ends and does not assert a surveyed pavement polygon. Field codes in
source details remain original; airport elevation is not an airspace floor/ceiling. Refer to FAA's
original specification for type-dependent units. These references never prove absence of restrictions.

## Controlled and special-use airspace

Choose the advertised **Class Airspace** or **Special Activity Airspace** original under NASR, review
the whole-original transfer/storage allowances, approve and import after completion. The larger Class
original requires an allowance above its published size. Original source files are preserved. Import
adds aviation database space; it does not use the scientific database or award mission credit.

Enable individual Class B/C/D/E or special-use categories/editions in Overlays & Legend. Polygon
viewports are bounded to **12 degrees** per side, **100** bounding candidates/page and **25,000**
returned display vertices; zoom closer or use the paged list. Holes and multipart shapes are retained.
Display generalization, original vertex count, source CRS, lower/upper units and datum are inspectable.
An unknown or negative source sentinel is **Unknown**, never a zero-altitude clearance.

The current SAA reader supports literal CRS84 polygons, standalone full circles and the verified partial-arc subset in one BASE volume. Unsupported curved boundaries,
SUBTR/UNION/multiple volumes and contributor relationships are counted as unsupported and not drawn;
their verified originals remain available. Source hours, DST and UTC offsets are retained verbatim.
Published H24 or a definition-validity date does **not** establish live activation. Review each dataset's
omissions and schedule uncertainty; no overlay can establish that an area has no restrictions.

### Versioned SAA circle and partial-arc geometry

Re-select **Import FAA Reference Features** on an already verified SAA download to create the
current derived interpretation without transferring its originals again. Schema 5 removes only the
one-interpretation-per-job constraint; a verified WAL-aware schema-4 backup and all existing geometry
remain. Each interpretation has its own ID and selectable overlay. Mission schema 8 and science schema 3
are unchanged. Reopening with older software requires the retained compatible backup, not a schema restamp.

The SAA adapter now renders standalone, explicitly georeferenced full circles in NM/MI/FT/M using
WGS84 geodesics with a 0.5-metre nominal chord target. Reference details distinguish sampled rendered
vertices from original center/radius values; original XML/hashes remain authoritative. This is a
rendering target, not certified FAA positional accuracy. Adapter 3 additionally supports explicit
increasing minor arcs (up to 180 degrees) between adjacent original literal segments. CRS84 angles
are interpreted from the longitude axis. Derived endpoints use the original literal vertices only
when independently within 0.5 m; larger gaps, angular wraps, reversed/major spans, adjacent arcs,
unsupported dateline/polar bounds and invalid topology remain explicit omissions. Original XML is
unchanged. Reimport keeps earlier interpretations and needs no new download or schema migration.
Composite vertical operations and contributor relationships remain unsupported. Live activation is Unknown.

TFR boundary details and notices without geometry link directly to the selected official FAA notice
in a separate browser tab. Review that original text for its actual restrictions. AeroScience's map
and screening still retain Unknown activation/altitude/segment states where structured authoritative
evidence is unavailable; opening the link does not establish an all-clear or modify your mission.
