# v0.4.0 replay and coverage

Open a completed/interrupted mission in Mission History. The workspace contains Overview, Replay, Coverage, Imagery, Analysis and Export. It works without MSFS or internet when the selected archive/library are available.

Replay uses indexed pages of actual stored samples (200 default, 500 maximum), UTC seeking, previous/next samples and 0.5×/1×/2×/5×/10× playback. The map and readings follow saved packets. Events, acquisitions, markers and notes offer timestamp jumps. Instrument configuration is the last archived effective snapshot at/before the selected time; legacy missions without an initial snapshot report that limitation instead of projecting their final settings backwards. Near-time scientific observations/acquisitions retain their own timestamps and source IDs; a ±3-second window is explicitly identified. Missing time is not interpolated into scientific observations. Playback never controls MSFS.

Coverage distinguishes:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Planned-route adherence | Existing comparison of recorded flight to the ordered intended route; it is not scientific coverage. |
| Modeled geometric coverage | Union of actually saved acquisition footprints clipped to the saved survey polygon union. Source availability alone does not contribute. |
| Valid scientific support | Union of finite, QA-filtered saved native source pixels intersected with each acquisition footprint and survey. NDVI requires intersecting red/NIR support. Repeated observations do not inflate area. |
| Gaps | Survey minus the corresponding geometric or valid-support union. The two gap layers answer different questions. |

Per-source results keep scenes/dates/units/datums distinct. Overall valid-data support is a geographic union only, not an image blending independently dated measurements. With no survey polygon, absolute approximate areas are available and percentages are **unavailable**. A fully filled display rectangle does not establish full valid support.

The vector analysis uses a regional WGS84 azimuthal-equidistant plane derived with GeographicLib, restricted to a 100 km radius. Its approximate area distortion is at most 0.005% within that radius; pixel edges are corner chords and footprint/terrain uncertainty is additional. It uses NetTopologySuite polygon union/intersection rather than an area-inflating coarse raster. GeoJSON export splits antimeridian geometry. To resolve floating-point shared-edge round trips only, geographic output coordinates snap to 1e-10 degree (~0.012 mm), with an explicit area-change guard of perimeter×0.1 mm or 0.001 m²; reported local areas remain unsnapped.

Bounds: eight million saved valid values, 500,000 complete-cell index entries and one million accumulated geometry vertices. Exceeding a bound reports a failure rather than claiming a partial survey percentage. Interactive polygons above 8 MiB remain downloadable with a scalar summary instead of exhausting a tablet browser. All source scenes and their limitations remain traceable in the saved result and research package.

The Analysis tab offers bounded atmospheric/terrain charts, real gaps, cloud boundaries and direction/altitude-bin means. Chart thinning is disclosed; computations use actual observations. Shared display preferences change presentation, not saved canonical measurements.

Automated fixture and genuine-source checks are separate from physical iPad, VR and long Windows-flight acceptance. Follow [the Windows plan](v0.4.0-windows-testing.md).
