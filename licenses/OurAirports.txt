OurAirports airport search data
https://ourairports.com/data/
https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data

Terms of use published by OurAirports:
"All data is released to the Public Domain, and comes with no guarantee of accuracy or fitness for use."

AeroScience retains original geographic positions in a compact offline search index,
excluding closed airports and invalid coordinates. This community dataset is geographic
reference information, not certified aviation navigation. See the bundled
geography/airport-search-provenance.json for pinned source identity and checksums.
